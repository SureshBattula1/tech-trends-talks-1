```html

  <h1>Your SEO Title</h1>
</div>

<!-- Visually hidden -->
<div class="visually-hidden">
  Hidden content
</div>

<!-- Generic SEO only -->
<div class="seo-only">
  SEO content
</div>
```

All defined in `src/styles.scss` globally!

🧮 Technical Calculation Details

EMI Calculation Formula

EMI = [P × r × (1 + r)ⁿ] / [(1 + r)ⁿ - 1]
P = Principal Loan Amount
r = Monthly Interest Rate (Annual Rate ÷ 12 ÷ 100)
n = Total Number of Months

Affordability Check

Affordability Ratio = (EMI ÷ Disposable Income) × 100
Disposable Income = Monthly Salary - Existing EMIs
Safe Limit = 50% of Disposable Income
Result = Ratio ≤ 50% (Eligible)

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about loan eligibility and our calculator

How to check loan eligibility?

+

To check loan eligibility, enter your monthly income, desired loan amount, tenure, existing EMIs, and select loan type. Our calculator will instantly show your eligibility status and EMI.

What is the minimum salary required for loan eligibility?

+

The minimum salary requirement varies by loan type. Generally, ₹5,000 monthly income is required for basic eligibility, but specific loan types may have higher requirements.

How is loan eligibility calculated?

+

Loan eligibility is calculated based on your monthly income, existing EMIs, loan amount, tenure, and interest rate. We use the EMI formula and check if the EMI ratio is within acceptable limits.

What is a good EMI to income ratio?

+

A good EMI to income ratio is below 50%. Below 40% is excellent, 40-50% is good, and 50-60% is moderate. Above 60% may strain your finances.

Can I get a loan with low credit score?

+

Yes, you can get loans with a low credit score, but you may face higher interest rates or need to provide additional security. Consider secured loans like gold loans for better approval chances.

What documents are required for loan application?

+

Common documents include ID proof, address proof, income proof, bank statements, and employment details. Requirements vary by loan type and lender.

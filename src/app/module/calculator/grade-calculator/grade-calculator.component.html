📐 Calculation Formulas

SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average)

SGPA = (Σ Ci × Pi) / (Σ Ci)

Where:

  • Ci = Credit assigned to the i-th course
  • Pi = Grade point secured in the i-th course
  • n = Number of courses registered for examinations

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average)

CGPA = (Σ (SGPA)i × Ni) / (Σ Ni)

Where:

  • (SGPA)i = SGPA of i-th semester
  • Ni = Number of credits in i-th semester
  • k = Number of semesters completed

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How to calculate SGPA?

+

SGPA = (Σ Ci × Pi) / (Σ Ci) where Ci is credit assigned to i-th course and Pi is grade point secured in i-th course.

How to calculate CGPA?

+

CGPA = (Σ (SGPA)i × Ni) / (Σ Ni) where (SGPA)i is SGPA of i-th semester and Ni is number of credits in i-th semester.

What is the SASTRA grading system?

+

S (91-100%): Outstanding (10 points), A+ (86-90%): Excellent (9 points), A (75-85%): Very Good (8 points), B (66-74%): Good (7 points), C (55-65%): Satisfactory (6 points), D (50-54%): Pass (5 points), F (0-49%): Fail (0 points)

How to convert CGPA to percentage?

+

Multiply your CGPA by 10 to get the equivalent percentage. For example, CGPA 8.5 = 85%.

What is a good CGPA?

+

CGPA 8.0+ is considered excellent, 7.0-7.9 is good, 6.0-6.9 is satisfactory, and below 6.0 needs improvement.

How to improve my CGPA?

+

Focus on improving performance in core subjects, maintain consistent study habits, seek help for difficult courses, and consider reducing course load if necessary.

Can I calculate grades for multiple semesters?

+

Yes, our calculator supports multiple semesters. Add as many semesters as needed to calculate your overall CGPA.

How accurate is the grade calculator?

+

Our calculator uses the official SASTRA formulas and grading system, ensuring 100% accuracy in calculations.

Can I download my grade report?

+

Yes, you can download detailed reports in PDF and Excel formats for record keeping and academic planning.

What if I have failed courses?

+

Failed courses (F grade) contribute 0 grade points to your SGPA and CGPA. Address failed courses immediately to improve your academic standing.

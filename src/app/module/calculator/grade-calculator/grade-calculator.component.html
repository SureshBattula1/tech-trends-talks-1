🔍 About Grade Calculator

What is SGPA?

SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) is the average of grade points earned in all courses taken during a specific semester. It's calculated using the formula: SGPA = (Σ Ci × Pi) / (Σ Ci) where Ci is credits and Pi is grade points.

What is CGPA?

CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the average of SGPA across all semesters. It's calculated using: CGPA = (Σ SGPAi × Ni) / (Σ Ni) where SGPAi is the SGPA of the i-th semester and Ni is the number of credits.

Grade Conversion

Our calculator automatically converts your marks to the appropriate letter grades and grade points based on the standard 10-point grading scale. You can also convert CGPA to percentage by multiplying by 10.

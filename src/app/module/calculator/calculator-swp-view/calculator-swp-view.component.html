What is SWP Calculator?

A SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan) calculator is a financial tool that helps investors plan regular withdrawals from their investment corpus. It's essential for retirement planning, calculating sustainable monthly income, and managing your wealth during post-retirement years.

How SWP Calculator Works

The SWP calculator calculates how long your corpus will last with regular withdrawals. It considers your initial investment, monthly withdrawal amount, expected return rate, and withdrawal period to show total withdrawn amount, remaining corpus, and sustainability of your withdrawal plan.

Benefits of SWP

  • Regular Income: Get steady monthly income while keeping your corpus invested
  • Tax Efficiency: Only capital gains portion is taxed, not the entire withdrawal
  • Flexibility: Adjust withdrawal amounts based on your needs
  • Capital Appreciation: Remaining corpus continues to grow with market returns
  • Inflation Protection: Investment growth helps maintain purchasing power

When to Use SWP Calculator

  • Planning retirement income
  • Managing post-retirement corpus
  • Creating passive income stream
  • Planning children's education expenses
  • Managing lump sum inheritance or windfall

SWP vs SIP

SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) is for wealth accumulation where you invest regularly to build corpus. SWP (Systematic Withdrawal Plan) is for wealth distribution where you withdraw regularly from accumulated corpus. SIP is typically used during earning years, while SWP is used during retirement or when you need regular income.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum corpus required for SWP?

There's no fixed minimum, but ideally you should have at least 30-40 times your annual withdrawal amount to ensure sustainability. For ₹10,000 monthly withdrawal, aim for ₹36-48 lakhs corpus.

How is SWP taxed?

Only the capital gains portion of your withdrawal is taxable. For equity funds, LTCG above ₹1 lakh is taxed at 10%, and STCG at 15%. For debt funds, gains are added to your income and taxed as per your slab.

Can I change my SWP amount?

Yes, most mutual funds allow you to modify SWP amount, pause it, or stop it anytime. Check with your fund house for specific terms and notice periods.

What is a sustainable SWP withdrawal rate?

Financial experts suggest 4-6% annual withdrawal rate from your corpus is sustainable. This rate helps balance regular income needs with corpus longevity and inflation protection.

Which funds are best for SWP?

Balanced or hybrid funds with 60-70% equity are ideal for SWP as they provide growth to beat inflation while offering stability. Pure equity funds work well for longer tenures (15+ years).

How accurate are SWP calculator results?

SWP calculator provides estimates based on assumed return rates. Actual results may vary due to market fluctuations. It's recommended to assume conservative returns (8-10%) for realistic planning.
